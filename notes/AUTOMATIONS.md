# Automations worth setting up

Do not turn these on until the ticket system can send a real status. A reminder that lies is worse than no reminder.

## When the portal exists

1. Lodged email and SMS. Text: received, not a call-out. Link back to the ticket.
2. Needs a look, if photos are missing after one day. Ask for the door shot and the wall shot.
3. Date offered, no tap in two days. Office follow-up list, not another automatic nag.
4. Day before a locked date. Confirm access, water if a wash is booked, and the booth colour block.
5. Report released. Ask for a Google review. Only after a reviewer has signed the report.
6. Due dates, once booths have them. 30 and 7 days for service. 3 days for filters. Link opens real slots only.

## Grok project automations, safe now

1. Monday note. Read `notes/DECISIONS.md` and list open questions still unanswered. Do not invent prices.
2. On a push to this repo, summarise what changed in `notes/` and say if a decision was edited without a new row.

Spam Act: service messages about a booked job are fine. Campaigns need consent and an unsubscribe.
